Website notice

Privacy and accessibility, in plain language.

This notice describes the public marketing website. It does not replace the practice's HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices or its medical-record policies.

The short version

This website is for general public information. Do not use it to send medical, billing, or other private information.

Call the office
01

Information on this website

The public pages provide information about the practice, staff, services, results, location, and ways to contact or schedule with the office. The website does not currently include a patient intake form, patient portal, or public medical-message form.

02

Technical information

Like most websites, hosting and security providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as browser, device, network, request, and error information. This information should not be used to submit medical details.

03

External services

Links to Square or map services open services operated by other companies. Their privacy practices and account protections apply when you use them. Use only the office's official public booking link.

04

Patient and medical privacy

Do not send protected health information, symptoms, medical history, insurance information, identification documents, or payment-card details through ordinary website channels. Contact the office and follow its secure instructions for medical records, patient forms, and private communication.

05

Accessibility

The goal is to make this website understandable and usable with keyboard navigation, readable text, meaningful headings, visible focus states, alternative text, and responsive layouts. If something prevents you from accessing information, call the office so another format or method can be provided.

06

Questions or updates

Website practices may change as features are added. For questions about this website, accessibility assistance, or the practice's separate privacy procedures, contact the office at (219) 728-6562.

For emergencies, call 911. This website is not monitored for urgent medical concerns.
